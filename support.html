Getting started

Open the included sample vault or choose a folder for your planning files. Add your teaching periods and repeating classes when you are ready.

Files and iCloud

PlanWell stores your planning data as Markdown files in a folder you control. Choose an iCloud Drive folder if you want the files available on your other Macs.

Calendar and Reminders

Calendar and Reminders access is optional. Enable only the items you want to sync, and change either permission later in macOS System Settings.

Menu bar and widgets

If information looks out of date, open PlanWell and confirm that it still has access to your planning folder.

Privacy

No account is required and PlanWell does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking. Read the full privacy policy.

Contact

For support, feedback, or feature requests, email contact@planwellmd.com. Include your macOS version and a short description of what happened.

Obsidian and Markdown

Choose your Obsidian vault as the PlanWell folder to use the same planning files in both apps. You can also open the files in any text editor.
