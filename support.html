Getting started

Open PlanWell and choose a folder for your planning files. You can start with the included sample vault, then move to a folder you control when ready.

Your files

PlanWell stores planning data as local markdown files. The files remain readable outside the app and can be opened in editors such as Obsidian.

Calendar and reminders

Calendar and Reminders access is optional. You can enable or disable each integration in PlanWell settings and change access later in macOS System Settings.

Widgets and menu bar

If a widget is out of date, open PlanWell once and confirm that the app still has access to your selected planning folder.

Privacy

No account is required and PlanWell does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking. Read the full privacy policy.

Contact

For support, feedback, or feature requests, email contact@planwellmd.com. Include your macOS version and a short description of what happened.
