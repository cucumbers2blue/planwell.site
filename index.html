Google Calendar

Google mode

Your school runs on Google Calendar. Keep it.

Connect your Google account and choose which calendars PlanWell shows. Mark each one as Classes, which fill the periods they overlap, or Events, which sit above the timetable. Pick a Google Tasks list for your weekly goals.

  • Edits go straight back to Google
  • Shared staff calendars stay in sync
  • Your data is never sent to PlanWell

Markdown files

Local Markdown mode

Prefer plain files? Those work too.

Keep every class, event and todo as an ordinary .md file in a folder you choose. Open it in Obsidian, back it up anywhere, and sync with Apple Calendar and Reminders if you like. No account needed.
