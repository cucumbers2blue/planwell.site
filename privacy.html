Privacy first

Privacy policy

No analytics. No tracking. No telemetry. Your planning files stay under your control.

Last updated: August 20, 2026

What we collect

The app collects nothing. We do not receive personal information, lesson content, or usage data from PlanWell.

Where data lives

PlanWell stores your planning data in Markdown files you control.

  • Files stay in folders you choose.
  • You can use iCloud Drive or another backup method.
  • You can open or migrate files anytime.

Calendar and Reminders

PlanWell for Mac uses Calendar and Reminders access only when you enable sync and grant permission. This work happens on your Mac, and PlanWell does not send the information to us.

Website tracking

planwellmd.com does not run analytics scripts or tracking pixels. No pageview or click instrumentation is active.

Third-party services

No ad networks, behavior analytics, or telemetry processors are used for core operation.

Platform note

This policy applies to PlanWell for Mac and PlanWell Web. The web app uses browser file access, so your content stays in the files you select.
