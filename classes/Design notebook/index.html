What is a digital footprint?

The trail you leave every time you go online every post comment photo like and search leaves a trays called your digital footprint

  • it can be seen by people who have never met
  • it can be copied and shared without your permission.
  • It can still exist even after you delete the original post
  • it can affect how people see you now or in the future.
