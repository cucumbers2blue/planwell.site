The Questions

Part 1: You, before this unit

1. Name a film scene where the music really affected you. What did the music do?

2. What do you already know about how film music works? (Themes, orchestras, sound design... anything.)

Part 2: Reading the Unit Plan

3. Find the statement of inquiry in the unit plan. Copy it here.

4. Now write it in your own words. Can music really change what we see?

5. Pick one inquiry question from the plan that interests you most. Why that one?

6. Task A is a presentation package about one approved scene. What three things does it contain, and what rule must your scene follow? (Look in the plan.)

Part 3: Thinking ahead

7. Why do you think we analyse film music instead of only "normal" music? Your best guess.

8. This unit practises audio-visual analysis and arguing with timestamped evidence. Where else in school or life could those skills help you?

9. What do you expect to learn from this unit? What do you hope we do in class?

10. Anything you are worried or confused about? (Scene choice? The presentation? Be honest.)
