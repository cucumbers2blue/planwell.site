Grade 6 Music - Task A: Reading a Piano Score

What This Task Is About

Musical symbols tell musicians what to play.

You will study one short piano song.

You will redraw the score and explain its symbols.

Statement of Inquiry

Musicians use pulse and simple symbols to play rhythm together.

What You Will Do

Your teacher will give you a short piano score.

1. Look carefully at the complete score.

2. Redraw the score on paper.

3. Use colour to show important symbols and elements.

4. Label the symbols chosen by your teacher.

5. Explain what each symbol tells the musician.

Your drawing does not need to be artistic.

It needs to be clear and musically accurate.

What to Annotate

Your teacher will choose the elements that appear in the score.

These may include:

  • staff
  • clef
  • key signature
  • time signature
  • bar line
  • notes and rests
  • repeat sign
  • dynamics

For each chosen element:

1. Circle or colour it in the score.

2. Write its name.

3. Explain what it tells the musician.

Helpful Sentence

“This is a [name]. It tells the musician [meaning].”

Check Before You Finish

  • [ ] I redrew the complete short score.
  • [ ] My notes and symbols are in the correct places.
  • [ ] I labelled every element chosen by my teacher.
  • [ ] I explained what each element tells the musician.
  • [ ] My colours make the score easier to understand.
  • [ ] My name is on my work.

What You Hand In

Hand in your annotated paper score.

You will begin this task before midterm preparation.

You will finish and review it after the midterm exams.

How This Is Marked

| Level | What your work shows |

| --- | --- |

| 1-2 | I copy part of the score and identify a few elements with help. |

| 3-4 | I copy most of the score and give simple names or explanations. |

| 5-6 | I redraw the score clearly and accurately explain its main elements. |

| 7-8 | I redraw the score accurately and clearly explain how its elements help a musician perform it. |
