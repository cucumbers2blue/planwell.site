Grade 6 Music — Formative A: Find It in the Unit Plan

Why We Do This

This is not graded.

This activity helps you practise opening and reading the Grade 6 Unit Plan.

Every answer is written in the Unit Plan.

What You Need

  • A piece of paper and a pencil
  • The Grade 6 Unit Plan in The Pantry

If you can open your Google Doc without help, you may type your answers instead.

What You Will Do

1. Open The Pantry.

2. Open the Grade 6 Unit Plan.

3. Find each answer in the Unit Plan.

4. Write only the question number and your answer.

You do not need to copy the questions.

The Five Questions

1. What is the title of this unit?

2. What is the key concept?

3. Copy the Statement of Inquiry.

4. Copy one conceptual inquiry question.

5. Copy the debatable inquiry question.

Before You Finish

  • [ ] I opened the Grade 6 Unit Plan.
  • [ ] I answered all five questions.
  • [ ] I found every answer in the Unit Plan.
  • [ ] My name is on my paper or Google Doc.

How I'll Use This

I will use this activity to see who can open, read, and find information in the Unit Plan.
